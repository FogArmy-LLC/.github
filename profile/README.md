## What FogArmy Builds

FogArmy delivers **Autonomous Company & Engineering Operations Systems (`ZTOS`)** that replace fragile prompt wrappers and per-seat SaaS sprawl with **3-Gate Adversarially Verified Shipping** and **Isolated Tenant Appliances**.

### 1. Pack 1 · Ship Product (Autonomous Software & Operations Delivery)
- **3-Gate Merge Verification:** Every change is gated by **(1) Deterministic Build & Test Execution**, **(2) Cross-Vendor Adversarial Review on the Rendered Surface**, and **(3) Blast-Radius & Caller/Reader/Writer Parity Verification**.
- **14-Day Code Survival Telemetry:** Every merged pull request is tracked across a rolling 14-day survival window to measure real production durability rather than raw commit volume.
- **Real-Time Operational Symmetry:** Multi-agent engineering, verification, and release strata coordinate across isolated worktrees and dedicated tenant appliances with cryptographic audit receipts.

### 2. Pack 2 · Grow Pipeline (Multi-Channel Social & Omnichannel ZTOPS)
- **Unified Publishing, Omnichannel Inbox & Attribution:** Consolidate multi-channel scheduling (`social.fogarmy.com`), unified web live-chat & social DM/comment triage (`inbox.fogarmy.com`), workflow automation (`hooks.fogarmy.com`), and branded UTM shortlink attribution (`go.fogarmy.com`).
- **Unlimited Team Seats & 1-Click Human Takeover:** Flat workspace pricing with AI Concierge triage and instant 1-click human operator takeover.
